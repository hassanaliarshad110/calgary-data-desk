Ek modem se shuru hua Bharat ka pehla game studio... pahuncha kahaan? 🎮 1997, Bengaluru: Rajesh Rao ne Dhruva Interactive shuru kiya — jise Bharat ka pehla game studio maana jaata hai. Asli shuruaat 1995 mein: bank loan, papa se mile paise, ek computer aur 14.4 kbps ka modem. Pehla game contract 1998 mein mila (Infogrames) — lekin wo game kabhi release hi nahi hua! Phir Dhruva ne duniya ke bade games ke art mein haath bataya: Forza, Halo, Sea of Thieves.
