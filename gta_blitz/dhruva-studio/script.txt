h1o: Ek modem se shuru hua Bharat ka pehla game studio... pahuncha kahaan?
n01: 1997, Bengaluru. Rajesh Rao ne shuru kiya Dhruva Interactive, jise Bharat ka pehla game studio maana jaata hai.
n02: Asli shuruaat 1995 mein: bank loan, papa se mile paise, ek computer aur 14.4 kbps ka modem.
n03: Pehla game contract 1998 mein mila. Lekin wo game kabhi release hi nahi hua!
n04: Phir Dhruva ne duniya ke bade games ke art mein haath bataya: Forza, Halo, Sea of Thieves.
n05: Twist: 2019 mein GTA banane wali Rockstar Games ne Dhruva ko khareed liya, 7.9 million dollar mein!
n06: Kareeb 300 log Rockstar India mein shaamil ho gaye. Ek modem se GTA wali company tak!
