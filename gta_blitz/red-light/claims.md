# Claims table — GTA blitz #25: Red light jump in GTA vs India: challan aur points (FACT)

Sources read this run (web-fetch agents):
- A: https://courtbook.in/motor-vehicles-act-1988/section-184 — MV Act 1988 s.184 as amended by Act 32 of 2019, s.67 (w.e.f. 1-9-2019): "Whoever, drives a motor vehicle at a speed or in a manner which is dangerous to the public ... shall be punishable for the first offence with imprisonment for a term which may extend to one year but shall not be less than six months or with fine which shall not be less than one thousand rupees but may extend to five thousand rupees, or with both, and for any second or subsequent offence if committed within three years of the commission of a previous similar offence, with imprisonment for a term which may extend to two years, or with fine of ten thousand rupees, or with both." Explanation (inserted 2019): "(a) jumping a red light; (b) violating a stop sign; (c) use of handheld communications devices while driving; ... shall amount to driving in such manner which is dangerous to the public."
