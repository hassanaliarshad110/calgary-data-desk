## Post-publication correction (time zone)
Alberta has been on year-round "Alberta Time" (UTC-6) since fall 2026, so 16:46 UTC = 10:46 a.m. local, not 9:46 MST. The posted reel's small source line under the warning scene reads "(updated 9:46 MST)"; the post was already being published when this was caught (Metricool 409, cannot edit). Every figure is unaffected. Corrected everywhere else: scene.js source line ("updated 10:46 a.m. Alberta time"), the site video (re-rendered), the site card, Data Genie's published fact, and the package.
