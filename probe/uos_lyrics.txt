[Verse 1 – English rap]
Before the towers ever kissed the sky,
Mohkinstsis — where the Bow and Elbow tie,
Blackfoot, Tsuut'ina, Stoney Nakoda, Métis land,
Treaty 7 ground — we stand here, hand in hand.
Eighteen seventy-five, a fort beside the water,
Then the railroad rolled in every son and daughter,
Ranchers and the dreamers, oil beneath the ground,
Chinook wind blowing, turn the winter all around.
Nineteen eighty-eight, the whole world came to see,
Olympic flame burning in the heart of YYC,
Twenty-thirteen, the river took the street —
We grabbed a shovel, neighbours, back up on our feet!
