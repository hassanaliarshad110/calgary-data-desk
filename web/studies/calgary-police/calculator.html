What research links to that change

Property crime per residentViolent crime per resident
Read this before quoting the crime figures. They are not a forecast for Calgary. They apply the range found in studies of police numbers elsewhere: a 10% rise in officers per resident went with 1.7% to 3% less property crime (Chalfin and McCrary, 242 US cities, 1960 to 2010; Bradford's review for the UK police inspectorate, 2011) and about 3.4% less violent crime (Chalfin and McCrary; some other studies found a smaller effect on violent crime, or none). Crime has many causes; the effect depends on what officers do; it may differ for cuts and increases; and Calgary's own crime severity fell from 2019 to 2025 while its officers per resident also fell. Use the range to understand the research, not to predict next year.
