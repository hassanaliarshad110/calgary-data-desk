Year by year, a month

2026 and 2027 use the City's tax rate and water rates. 2028 to 2030 are estimates: the property tax increases reported in the news (10%, 7.7%, 6%) and the water bill rising 74% above 2026 by 2030, spread evenly; waste held at 2027. They assume your assessment moves with the typical home after 2027.

How this is worked out. 2026 City tax: your assessed value times the 2026 municipal residential rate (0.0038906). 2027: your 2027 value times the rate that gives the City's own 2027 figure for the median home ($266 a month on $688,000), an estimate until Council sets the 2027 tax rate in spring 2027. Water: the City's 2026 and 2027 rates for a 15 mm meter; wastewater is charged on 88% of the water you use. Figures change if Council amends the budget.
Part of What Calgarians pay, an independent study by Calgary Insight. Sources: The City of Calgary, 2027 Budget, 2026 and 2027 water rates, residential waste rates, 2026 property tax bylaw.
