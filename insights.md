### Top 3 and bottom 3

- **Top 3: heat reel (10,915), Almost Fort Brisebois (2,672), Library over the CTrain (1,197).** What they share: one concrete surprise about something every Calgarian knows (today's weather, the city's own name, the downtown library), said in one short sentence with no numbers; a strong picture at frame 0; and an Instagram 3-second skip rate under 40% (the only other videos under 40% were Plus 15 and the war-memorial clones). All three went out Saturday-Sunday. Heat reel: 14 shares per 1,000 reached on Instagram, 13 saves; Brisebois: 10 saves and the longest Instagram watch of the week (19.5 s).
- **Bottom 3 (leaving out the YouTube-only Origins film): Military story (102), Pipe Under the Bow (133), Calgary cloned a war memorial (151).** The two documentaries were ~2-minute films with a headline/"hiding in plain sight" opening, posted on overloaded days (Nov 9 had 4 posts within 35 min at 23:05-23:40; Nov 11 had 9 Facebook posts); Military lost 91% of Instagram viewers in 3 s. The war-memorial clones reel is different: good hook (38% skip, 13 s watch on Instagram) but Facebook showed it to almost nobody (5 views), like Crowchild (8) and the Military teaser (9) the evening before. That looks like Facebook throttling the page after the Nov 11 burst, not weak content.

### Findings in plain words

1. **One lived moment beat everything else combined on Instagram.** The heat reel brought 58% of all Instagram views. Same-day "you felt this today" topics are the strongest lever we have (section 9 is working).
2. **Money and bills don't hold people in the first 3 seconds** (median 83% skip on Instagram, 234 views). Even "your bills" hooks failed when the picture at frame 0 was a chart or a headline. Facebook tolerates them better than Instagram (What Calgarians pay: 333 on FB, 34 on IG).
3. **Short wins for fun facts:** ≤25 s videos had the best median (655) and lowest skip (38%); 26-35 s videos had a median of 234 and 67% skip.
4. **Flagship Shorts work when the opening is a twist, not a summary** (Brisebois 39% skip vs Military 91%).
5. **Bunching kills reach.** Several posts within an hour, or 9 Facebook posts in a day, left the later ones with almost no Facebook views. The 60-minute gap in section 7 matters.
6. **YouTube is still tiny** (784 views, 3% of the week). Fun-fact Shorts with a "Really." twist did best there (WestJet 281).
7. Comments stay near zero (max 3 per video). The one-word questions aren't yet pulling replies.

### What to change

- Pick fun facts that are a twist on a place or name everyone knows; keep them 15-25 s; open on the place itself, not a map or text card.
- Make same-day moments a priority whenever section 9's test is met; they go to all three platforms.
- Money/budget videos: open on a picture of a house/street/bill and a "you" line, not a chart; keep them mainly for Facebook and YouTube (0c).
- Flagship Shorts: first line is the twist ("Calgary was almost called…"), never the theme ("…story hiding in plain sight").
- Never let several posts go out together: keep 60+ minutes between all posts and no more than ~4 Facebook posts a day; Origins parts at their own 10:00 slots (this week all four went out at 04:50 together).
- Calendar: swapped two upcoming rows that look like this week's bottom 3 (see below).
