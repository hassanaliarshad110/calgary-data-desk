He had his enemy beaten... and he dropped his sword. Why?

In a story by Rumi, Ali is winning a fight against an enemy chief, and the beaten man spits in his face:
"'Ali, instead of taking vengeance on him, at once dropped his sword, to the Magian's great astonishment."
— Masnavi, Book I, Story XVI (E. H. Whinfield's prose summary)

In essence, he fought for something higher than himself, not for his pride. The spit was the enemy's last weapon: he couldn't win the fight, so he tried to win Ali's temper.

Try this today: next time someone pokes at you, wait one breath and ask, is this my move, or theirs?
