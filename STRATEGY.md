YouTube tested Ep 5 next to big kids' videos. Kids clicked (3% is fine), but a third left inside the first few seconds and the average viewer was gone by 0:21. YouTube read that as "viewers don't like this channel" and stopped testing: Ep 6 got a smaller test, Ep 7 none.
Cause: every episode opened with a narrator recap ("Mama ko nahi pata… ye nanha sa Qasim hi hai…"), a "thodi der pehle" rewind and a setup scene (family in the car, etc.). The real problem appeared at 0:20–0:40.
Note: Made for Kids turns off comments and the subscriber bell, so every view depends on YouTube recommending the video. Retention is everything.

## The format now
- **Main product:** full 16:9 episode, 1:45–2:15.
- **Cadence:** new episodes Mon / Wed / Fri at 18:30 PKT (YouTube), Reels 19:35 PKT. Day after each episode: one cliffhanger Short (YouTube Short 18:30, Reels 19:35). Sunday: the weekly compilation. No daily episodes.
- **Instagram/Facebook** keep working on the secret hook (IG reels that led with the secret got 914 and 1,701 views); captions may lead with the secret line.

## Episode structure (seconds)
| Time | Beat |
|---|---|
| 0–5 | COLD OPEN: frame 0 is the danger or funniest chaos, in motion, with a hit/whoosh. A character IN the scene shouts the problem in kid words. A 2–4 word Roman title on screen. |
| 5–25 | Why it matters + family/Basim reaction, fast. Context in one line while the danger is on screen. |
| 25–40 | "Mama! Suusuu aayi hai!" → "Phir se?!" + Sakina's crying act. |
| 40–48 | Door glow, FLUUSH, SUUSUU POWER, "SUUU-SUUU-MAN!" (≤ 8 s; one of only two narrator lines, the other is the closing lesson). |
| 48–95 | The rescue, with ONE escalation around 1:05 (it gets worse before it gets better). |
| 95–110 | Secret-keeping joke ("Kuch nahi Mama!", Sakina's wink) + one-line lesson. |
| 110–115 | CLIFFHANGER: 3–4 s flash of the NEXT episode's danger, "AGLE EPISODE MEIN…". |
| last 12–15 s | End screen: Susuman waving on the left, room for YouTube's video + subscribe elements on the right. **The episode ENDS WITH THE LESSON** (Hassan, Nov 20, 2026): the first line on the end screen is the narrator's lesson, "Bachcho, <one-line lesson>!" (shown big on screen too), then "Video achhi lagi to LIKE aur SUBSCRIBE karo!". Exactly as Ep 1–8 ended. |

The closing lesson is MANDATORY in every episode, and it must be the last thing said before LIKE/SUBSCRIBE (after the cliffhanger flash, never only in the middle). A character may also say the lesson inside the story, but the narrator still repeats it on the end screen. The narrator may speak only twice: the SUUU-SUUU-MAN power line and this closing lesson.

Never: a narrator in the first 15 s, a recap of who Qasim is, a title card or theme song before 0:30, a "getting ready / going somewhere" scene before the problem, anything static for more than 3 s.

## Titles and thumbnails
- Title: `<Devanagari danger hook> 😱 <Roman keyword> | Susuman Ep N | Hindi Urdu Cartoon for Kids` (≤ 100 chars). Example: `गाय ने रोका पूरा शहर! 😱 Gaay Wala Cartoon | Susuman Ep 8 | Hindi Urdu Cartoon for Kids`.
- Thumbnail 1280x720: Susuman big on the left (same pose + crown every time), the animal/kid in danger big on the right, max 2 bold Roman words, bright, readable at phone size; no busy shop signs or small text.

## Shorts
Cliffhanger Short 25–40 s: open on the danger at frame 0, run through the escalation, END UNRESOLVED with "Poora episode dekho!". No recap at the start.

## Self-check before publishing
List what is on screen each second for the first 15 s of the final file: danger visible from frame 0, a character (not the narrator) speaks within 2 s, nothing static > 3 s. Contact sheet at 0, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 12 s.

## Manual YouTube Studio steps (Hassan; no API for these)
1. Playlist "Susuman – Saare Episodes" in story order; add every episode.
2. End screen on every episode: "Best for viewer" video + Subscribe, in the last 12–15 s.
3. After 48 h, check each new episode's Reach (impressions) and Engagement (average view duration). Goal: average % viewed ≥ 45%.
