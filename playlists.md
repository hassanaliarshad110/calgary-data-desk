# Calgary Insight playlists

Every Calgary Insight video, sorted by series so it can be added to its playlist. Metricool and vidIQ cannot add videos to playlists, so this is done by hand: YouTube in YouTube Studio (Content > select videos > Add to playlist), Facebook in Meta Business Suite (Content > Videos > Playlists). Instagram has no playlists for Reels; there, the series hashtag on every caption groups them.

Tick a box once the video is in its playlist. Each posting run adds its new videos under "To add" in the right series (PLAYBOOK section 7c).

Built Nov 26, 2026 from log.json and Metricool.
