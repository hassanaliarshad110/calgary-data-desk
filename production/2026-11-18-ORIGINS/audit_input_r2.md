# Audit input: Calgary Origins Ep 2 (ORIGINS 2026-11-18), revised script

## 1. Title and hooks
**Grass for a Penny: Calgary's Ranch Era** (YouTube: “Grass for a Penny: How Ranching Built Calgary | Calgary Origins”)

Chapter hooks (each part opens on a moving visual hook; first word on screen at frame 0):
- Part 1 “Grass for a Penny”: spoken “In eighteen eighty-one, Ottawa started renting out prairie grass for one cent an acre.” · on screen “GRASS FOR A PENNY” · visual: Low dawn drone skimming tall prairie grass toward the Rockies, wind combing the grass, warm side light; a single gold coin spins into frame and lands on the grass. One continuous move, 4 s.
- Part 2 “Two Hard Winters”: spoken “Alberta's first big ranch lost thousands of cattle in just two winters.” · on screen “TWO WINTERS. THOUSANDS LOST.” · visual: Blizzard over open range, snow streaking past the lens, dark cattle shapes hunched against the wind. 4 s.
- Part 3 “The Beef King”: spoken “Calgary's first millionaire built his fortune on beef.” · on screen “CALGARY'S FIRST MILLIONAIRE?” · visual: Dusk drone low over the Elbow River in today's Calgary toward downtown towers; a gold title punches in. 4 s.
- Part 4 “The End of the Open Range”: spoken “One winter, the chinook never came.” · on screen “NO CHINOOK.” · visual: A chinook arch that never opens: grey lid of cloud over a frozen range, snow blowing. 4 s.

Alternatives considered: “Calgary’s first millionaire was a meat packer.” / “The Sundance Kid broke horses southwest of Calgary.” / “Live cattle once mattered more to Canada than wheat.”
