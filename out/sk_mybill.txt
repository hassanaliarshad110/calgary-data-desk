About MyProperty 
 Welcome to MyProperty 
MyProperty is the City’s new secure self-service portal where you can access official City information, including property tax, assessment and utility billing in one convenient online location. 
    Accessing your official City notices online through MyProperty is optional . 
If you prefer to continue receiving paper copies, no action is required. 
 Setting up your new MyProperty account is easy.  
 All you need to get started is your 10-digit personal access code. 
You can find your personal access code on your: 
2026 property tax notice 
2026 assessment notice, including supplementary and amended notices 
Utility bills issued from May 2026 onward 
eBill notifications issued from August 2026 onward 
 Can’t find your access code? 
Call our Corporate Revenue Contact Centre at 306-975-2400 . We will verify your identity and help you get started with your new MyProperty account. 
Set up secure, convenient online access with your new MyProperty account. 
Helpful Sign-in Information for Our Existing eBill Customers 
Your previous My Utility Account username and password will not work to sign in to MyProperty. 
    To continue accessing detailed utility billing information online, you will need to create a new MyProperty account.

 Access MyProperty (My Utility Account)
 MyProperty gives you convenient online access to view, download and save your current and historical property tax, assessment and utility billing information. You can also: 
Delegate assessment and property tax access for owned properties, helpful for property managers​ 
Track utility consumption and set SmartUtil alerts​ 
View detailed assessment data, reports and comparisons with nearby-properties 
Apply for related services, including Home and Commercial Energy Loan tax deferral programs​ 
Share assessment and property tax information with authorized co-owners, tenants or property managers​ 
Manage information for multiple properties through one MyProperty account 
 Special message for existing eBill customers 
Your eBill will look different this fall. My Utility Account has been replaced by MyProperty, and My Utility Accoun t web pages are no longer active - this information is now under MyProperty on this website. Your utility billing information is now accessed through MyProperty. 
To continue accessing your full utility billing details and managing your account online, you will need to create a new MyProperty account using your unique access code. Your access code was included in your August 2026 eBill notification, please keep it handy for your account set up. You will only need to use the code once. 
Once you set up your MyProperty account, you can also choose to access your property tax and assessment notices and information online if you are a property owner.
 MyProperty Benefits  
Download and save official City notices, track and set alerts for utility consumption any time, 24/7, 365 
Receive your official City notices faster 
Reduced paper clutter 
Stay up to date and keep a record of past City notices through your secure MyProperty account 
 Other tax, assessment and utility self-service options available through MyProperty  
Download a PDF tax statement for a customer-specified date range 
Download assessment reports for owned properties 
Delegate access to your properties to an agent of your choosing, giving them access to view all details of your property including all tax and assessment notices 
Submit a meter read online. This service is only available to customers who have opted out of having an AMI smart meter. 
View the waste carts assigned to your property and request a different garbage cart size. 
Download various utility account reports in PDF or CSV/Excel format. 
Continued access to SmartUtil for electric and water utility smart meter detailed consumption data and ability to configure usage alerts or export available data. 
 Answering Your MyProperty Questions: log-in help, new account set-up, ebill-related, general 
 When can I start using MyProperty to access my property tax and assessment notices online, along with my utility billing notices? 
The MyProperty secure online portal is now open and you can set up your new MyProperty account with your 10-digit personal access code. 
 I created a new account for MyProperty, but now I need a 10-digit access code to see my information in MyProperty.  
 Where can I get my access code? 
Access codes for MyProperty were sent to residents through the following channels: 
On all paper property tax notices mailed in May 2026 
On all paper utility bills since June 2026 
On all electronic eBill notification emails since August 2026 
If you cannot locate your unique access code, please contact Corporate Revenue Customer Service at 306-975-2400 (M to F 8:30am to 4:30pm) or email revenue@saskatoon.ca for assistance. 
 I forgot my password for MyProperty. Can you help me sign in? 
If you have chosen to sign into MyProperty with an email address, you will receive a new one-time password each time you sign in. There is no password to set and remember. 
If you have chosen to log in with a Google or Apple account, you will access MyProperty using your regular login credentials for your selected third-party provider. These providers have their own processes for resetting passwords: 
Google Account recovery: https://www.google.com/accounts/recovery 
Apple Account recovery: https://support.apple.com/en-ca/apple-account 
Still having trouble? Contact Corporate Revenue Customer Service by calling 306-975-2400 (M to F 8:30am to 4:30pm) or emailing revenue@saskatoon.ca . 
 Can I use the account I already had for My Utility Account for MyProperty? 
No. Your My Utility Account credentials will not work when you try to access MyProperty. You will need to register for a new account to continue accessing your utility billing details. The sign-up process is quick and all you need is your 10-digit access code from your eBill notification or property notice to register. 
 How can I log in to SmartUTIL now that My Utility Accoun t is gone? 
Access to SmartUTIL has been relocated to MyProperty. You can register for MyProperty using your 10-digit access code. SmartUTIL is now accessed inside MyProperty. Remember, your previous My Utility Account credentials won't work to access MyProperty. 
 I am having a technical problem using MyProperty or wish to provide feedback about my experience using MyProperty.  
Customers are invited to email myproperty@saskatoon.ca to report technical problems or to provide feedback and help improve the service for everyone. 
 I have a question about the bills, notices, or information displayed inside of MyProperty. Who can I contact for help?  
All customer inquiries should be directed to Corporate Revenue Customer Service by calling 306-975-2400 (M to F 8:30am to 4:30pm) or emailing revenue@saskatoon.ca . 
 Do I have to use MyProperty to receive my property tax, assessment or utility notices? 
No. Using MyProperty to access your City notices online is optional. If you prefer to receive paper notices, you will continue to receive them and no action is required. 
 I'm currently on eBill. Do I have to sign up for MyProperty this fall? 
Your eBill notification will have a new look with MyProperty now LIVE. You will still receive a notification of your utility billing amount owing, and your preference for receiving your utility billing digitally will be carried over to MyProperty, however if you want to continue accessing the full details of your utility billing, you'll need so set up your MyProperty account using your personal access code. Your My Utility Account credentials will not work when you try to access MyProperty. 
Once your account is set up, you will need to link your utility account to your MyProperty profile to continue receiving your utility billing notices online. 
 Why do I need to sign up again if I am already on eBill? 
 The Cities Act requires a property owner to opt-in if they wish to receive online notices for utilities, assessment and property tax. 
 Will I be able to connect to more than one property? 
You will need a new access code if you want to connect to a different property.  
For example, if you use your unique access code to view your personal property and then want to access your business property, you will need to enter the access code from the property tax or utility billing notice sent for your business. 
 Can I view properties I do not own? 
MyProperty is intended for property owners and utility account holders. Users can view details only for properties they own. Property owners will be able to delegate access to an agent of their choosing. 
For access to property assessment and tax information related to other properties you'd like information on, please use the Property Assessment and Tax Tool , which provides publicly available information. 
 Why did the City create MyProperty? Why not continue with My Utility Account ? 
MyProperty was created to give residents a more convenient, secure and centralized way to access important City information online. Digital access to City information is something many residents have been requesting for years. 
While My Utility Account provided access to eBill and utility billing information, MyProperty expands that experience by bringing together property tax and assessment eNotices, detailed utility billing information, SmartUtil and more in one place. 
MyProperty also provides a foundation for the City to add more centralized online services over time, making it easier for residents to find and manage their City information. 
MyProperty is designed to grow with residents’ needs and our goal is to provide an enhanced, more connected online experience over time. 
By choosing online access to official City notices, residents can find and download the information they need when they need it, reduce paper clutter and manage their City information through one secure account. 
Residents who prefer paper notices can continue receiving them and do not need to take any action. 
 MyProperty Security and Privacy 
MyProperty uses trusted and secure technology, including encryption and leading cybersecurity protection to protect your personal information. 
Your access code is unique to your account; keep it safe and do not share it by email or on social media. 
All notices available through MyProperty are official and issued by the City of Saskatoon. 
To protect your information, the City will never ask you by email for your password, banking information or payment card details. 
 Questions or need help setting up your MyProperty account? 
Visit saskatoon.ca/mypropertyinfo for account set up tips and helpful information, email revenue@saskatoon.ca or call our Corporate Revenue Contact Centre at 306-975-2400 . We will be pleased to assist you.
