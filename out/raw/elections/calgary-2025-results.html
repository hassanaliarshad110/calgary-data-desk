2025 Results - Calgary General Election

Official results

The 2025 Calgary General Election official results are below. The mayor, councillor, and public and separate school board trustee elections took place on Oct. 20, 2025, in Calgary, Alberta, Canada.

Official results have been corrected following the Oct. 27, 2025 mayoral contest recount held under Section 98.1 of the Local Authorities Election Act.

voters

39.04%

Total voters - 349,815*

Total enumerated electors - 896,042**

*Estimated, as of Oct. 24, 2025. Revisions may occur following post-election review of voter registration information, including amendments to the City of Calgary Permanent Electors Register.

**Reference: City of Calgary Permanent Electors Register (data downloaded from the Elections Alberta elector dataset on Sept. 16, 2025).

Election terms

Elected by acclamation - when only one candidate is officially nominated by the close of nominations. That candidate is declared elected without the need for a vote. 
Enumerated electors - means eligible voters whose names are contained on the City of Calgary Permanent Electors Register. 
Incumbent - refers to the individual who currently holds a specific elected position.
