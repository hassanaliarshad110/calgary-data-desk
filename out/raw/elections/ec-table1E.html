Note:

Ordinary polls are those open on election day. They can be stationary (in a given location) or mobile. Mobile polls are used to collect votes at long-term care and senior residences and move from one institution to another. Advance polls are open between the 10th and 7th days before election day. Flexible advance polls may also be held during these days in remote, isolated or low-density communities for a reduced number of days and hours (based on community needs).

Actual number of voting desks based on the number of reported results; may have been slightly higher due to individual advance polls having the vote at multiple voting desks.
