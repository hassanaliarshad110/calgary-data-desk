2026 Water, Sewer & Infrastructure Rates
On January 1, 2026, Water, Sewer & Infrastructure rates will increase overall by 5.18% on
your utility bill.
Monthly Charges
The rate structure consists of fixed and volumetric charges. The fixed charge is based
on the water meter size to reflect the demand placed on the utilities. The volumetric
charge ($/cu. m.) is based on how much you use, to encourage conservation.
