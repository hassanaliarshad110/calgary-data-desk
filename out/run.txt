ANSWER:
Calgary has about **26.2 km of separated (protected) cycle tracks**. This is the figure city hall gave in July 2025. I found no newer City total.

- **Downtown:** 9.9 km of that is the downtown protected network, or 10.7 km if you include Mission. City hall has also used a larger figure of 20 km for downtown. That figure counts each direction as its own lane, so it isn't comparable.
- **Painted lanes:** Calgary also had 45.6 km of painted bike lanes at the same date.
- **Targets:** The 2011 Cycling Strategy aimed for 30 km of protected cycle tracks and 180 km of painted lanes by 2020. Cycle tracks are about 3.8 km short of the 30 km target.
- **Other routes:** The City lists about 290 km of on-street bikeways and cycle tracks combined. It also lists about 1,000 km of regional pathways, which are off-street, and 96 km of unpaved trails. These are different measures and shouldn't be added to the 26.2 km.

The 26.2 km comes from a news report quoting city hall, not from a City dataset. It may be out of date if tracks have opened since July 2025.
