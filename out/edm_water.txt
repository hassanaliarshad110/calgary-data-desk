Residential water and wastewater 
These rates are effective April 1, 2026. 
Most customers pay about $74 per month for water usage on an average, single-family residential household bill. This does not include fixed fees. This amount may also fluctuate month-to-month, depending on how much water is used.

 Stormwater charges 
Stormwater utility charges cover the cost to build, operate, and maintain the stormwater infrastructure, which collects accumulated rainwater and snowmelt to prevent flooding and property damage. 
 How is the Stormwater charge calculated?  
In accordance with the City of Edmonton EPCOR Wastewater Services Bylaw 20865 , the daily stormwater charge is calculated using the following formula: A x I x R x Rate , where:  
 A: Area  - The area of the property in square metres (m 2 ), and the proportion of the building lot area attributable to each unit for multiple units sharing a single building of property.  
 I: Development Intensity Factor -   The measure of the portion of lot being used for its intended development. The development intensity factor is 1.0 as default, except for commercial or multi-family residential properties where owners demonstrate they contribute significantly less stormwater runoff per property area to the City's sewer system during rainfalls than other similarly zoned properties by making an application for a reduction in the intensity development factor pursuant to the bylaw. 
 R: Runoff coefficient  - represents the portion of rainfall that becomes runoff and enters the stormwater system. The runoff coefficient relates to the portion of permeable and impermeable areas within your property and is determined based on the zoning.  
 Rate: The stormwater utility rate - the daily rate as of April 1, 2026 is $0.00327.

 Drainage services record search 
The cost of this service is $175.00 plus GST per titled lot and is invoiced the month after the record search is issued.
This search covers records for information related to the following sections of past and present City Bylaws: Sewers Bylaw 9425, Sections 4 – 38; Sewers Use Bylaw 9675, Sections 4 – 37; Drainage Bylaw 16200, Sections 4 – 40 and 50 – 51; Drainage Bylaw 18093, Sections 15 – 20; EPCOR Drainage Services Bylaw 18100, Schedule 2; EPCOR Water Services and Wastewater Treatment Bylaw 17698, Schedule 1, Part IV, Wastewater Overstrength Surcharges, EPCOR Drainage Services & Wastewater Treatment Bylaw 19627 Schedule 1 & 2, and 2 and EPCOR Wastewater Services Bylaw 20865 Schedule 1 & 2. These requests can be sent to  drainagerecordsearch@epcor.com  and require the following information: 
Municipal address and legal description; 
Customer address and contact information; 
Customer file or project number; and
Letter of authorization from the current property owner with clearly printed name and signature.​

 Sanitary and stormwater utility credits 
We recognize that some non-residential customers use a large volume of water that does not drain into the sewer system. These customers may apply for a non-contributive credit that would be applied to each month's sewer utility bill. The non-contributive credit would be calculated in direct proportion to the amount of water that is lost each month, averaged over an entire year.

The Stormwater Intensity Adjustment Program (formerly Stormwater utility credit) is an opportunity for commercial and multi-family residential customers in Edmonton to receive a credit on their monthly stormwater utility bill.
