Introduction
A user fee is a payment charged in exchange for a good or service provided by The City. User
fees are paid directly by those who benefit from a service and are an important part of
diversified municipal revenues, which help reduce reliance on property taxes and other revenue
sources used to provide City services. All proposed user fees are set in accordance with the
User Fee Policy (CP2024-02), which considers the full costs of providing the good or service,
market demand and the extent of public versus individual benefit derived.
The annual adjustments process allows for changes and updates to fees and rates. The Council
approval process for user fees involves:
1. Council approval of target long-term tax support rates (completed 2018 May)
This is the percentage of each service’s full costs that are expected to be recovered through
user fees. The long-term tax support rates approved by Council in report C2018-0586 on
2018 May 16 were used for setting user fees and rates for 2023-2026.
2. Council approval of detailed fees for 2023-2026 (completed 2022 November)
Council approved the specific rates and fees for each service for their four-year service
plans and budgets (C2022-1051).
3. Council approval of adjustments to fees
Currently approved fees for the 2023-2026 Service Plans Budgets include the original
approvals in 2022 November and subsequent adjustments in 2023 November (C2023-
1148). Where fees are proposed to change from what is currently approved, they are
reflected in this attachment:
• Adjustments to fees set by Council resolution will be approved by resolution.
• Adjustments to fees contained in bylaws will be approved by Council giving three
readings to the amending bylaws.

presented for information and summarizes changes that do not require Council approval
through this process.
