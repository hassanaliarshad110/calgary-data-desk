Start service in Edmonton 
Need utilities for your property in Edmonton? It's easy to set up electricity, natural gas, water and waste services — all on one convenient bill.

If you are currently a customer in Edmonton, you don't need to create a new account to transfer, switch or add services. 
 Moving? 
You can transfer your power, water and gas to your new address. Submit your move request at least 3-5 business days before your move date.

Understanding utility services in Edmonton 
In Edmonton, EPCOR and Encor by EPCOR provide different utility services and energy options: 
EPCOR provides water and wastewater services. 
EPCOR provides the Rate of Last Resort (RoLR), the default electricity option. 
Encor by EPCOR offers competitive electricity and natural gas plans. 
Learn more about the difference between  Encor vs EPCOR . 
Start service FAQs 
Questions about getting started? Let us help. 
 I'm already an EPCOR customer. Do I need a new account? 
No. If you're already an EPCOR customer, there's no need to open a new utility account. You can use your existing account to add a new service, switch services or  transfer your services within Edmonton. If you manage your account online, simply log in to get started. 
 What information do I need to start utility services? 
To set up service, you'll typically need your service address, requested start date and contact information. 
Current customers may also be asked for their account number. 
 How long does it take to start utility services? 
Most start service requests can be submitted online in just a few minutes. To ensure your services are ready when you need them, submit your request at least 3-5 business days before your requested start date. 
 Can I have all my utilities on one bill? 
Depending on your location and service eligibility in Edmonton, you may be able to set up water, electricity and natural gas services. Eligible services can be on one account, with one bill. Water service bills also include wastewater service and City of Edmonton waste collection charges. 
Customers are free to purchase natural gas services or electricity services from a retailer of their choice. For a list of retailers, visit ucahelps.alberta.ca 
 What's the difference between EPCOR and Encor? 
EPCOR provides water and wastewater services and the Rate of Last Resort (RoLR), the default electricity option in Edmonton. Encor by EPCOR offers competitive electricity and natural gas plans.  
Learn more about the difference between Encor vs EPCOR . 
 Can I choose a different energy retailer? 
Yes. Customers are free to purchase natural gas services or electricity services from a retailer of their choice. For a list of retailers, visit ucahelps.alberta.ca .
