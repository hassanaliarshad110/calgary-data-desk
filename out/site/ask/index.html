Data Genie: ask Calgary’s open data in plain words

Data Genie answers questions about Calgary from public data. It finds the right datasets among more than 400 from seven public sources, including the City of Calgary’s open data and Calgary Police Service statistics, works out the figures, and answers with charts, tables, maps, downloads and full reports. Every figure says where it comes from.

Who it is for

Councillors’ offices preparing casework, briefings and council meetings; community associations; journalists; researchers; and residents who want a straight answer about their part of the city.

What it can answer

  • Open 311 requests in any community, the oldest ones and what changed this year
  • Crime by community and kind, against last year
  • Growth, building permits and new homes
  • Comparisons between communities, wards and the whole city
  • Lists to download, such as every open request in a community

Where the figures come from

311 requests: The City of Calgary, 311 Service Requests open data (data.calgary.ca, iahh-g8bj), the last 12 complete months against the 12 before. Crime: Calgary Police Service, community crime statistics, the latest 12 months the police have published. Residents: City's total, shared by actual growth, with the 2021 Census count beside it. New homes and permits: City of Calgary building and development permit open data. Rates per 1,000 residents a year use residents in the middle of each period.
