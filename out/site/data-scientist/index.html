Data Scientist: full analysis reports on Calgary and any ward

Data Scientist writes complete, sourced reports from live public data: every figure worked out from the data itself, with charts, maps, findings, recommendations and an action plan. A second step, the report auditor, checks the figures and the reasoning before you read it.

What you get

  • A report as a PDF, ready to print or send
  • Interactive charts and maps
  • An Excel workbook with every figure behind the report

How a report is made

  1. Gather the data: the right public datasets for the question, for the area chosen
  2. Work out the figures: calculated from the records themselves, not estimated
  3. Draw charts and maps
  4. Write the report: findings in plain words, each figure with its source
  5. Audit: a separate check of the numbers, the method and the conclusions

Reports it is built for

  • How have vehicle thefts and break-ins changed in each community since 2021, with recommendations
  • Where are 311 requests taking longest to close, and which kinds
  • A briefing on a community before a town hall or council meeting
  • Growth and new housing against services in a ward
  • Your own question, described in plain words, with the visuals you want

Sample: Ward 5 in brief

Ward 5 has 148,087 residents by the City’s estimate. It had 33,015 311 requests in Nov 2025 to Oct 2026, 198 per 1,000 a year, and 4,832 requests are still marked open, 3,931 of them for over 90 days. Where the figures come from

311 requests: The City of Calgary, 311 Service Requests open data (data.calgary.ca, iahh-g8bj), the last 12 complete months against the 12 before. Crime: Calgary Police Service, community crime statistics, the latest 12 months the police have published. Residents: City's total, shared by actual growth, with the 2021 Census count beside it. New homes and permits: City of Calgary building and development permit open data. Rates per 1,000 residents a year use residents in the middle of each period.
