Coral Springs, Calgary

Coral Springs is a community in Ward 10 of Calgary, home to about 6,172 people (5,610 at the 2021 Census, up 10.0% since). Residents and businesses made 1,213 requests to 311 in Nov 2025 to Oct 2026, down 10.5% on the year before. 228 requests are open now, 205 of them for more than 90 days. The Calgary Police Service recorded 60 crimes here in Oct 2025 to Sep 2026 (9.7 per 1,000 residents a year; Calgary as a whole: 16.3), down 6.2% on the year before.

Crime in Coral Springs

60 crimes were reported to the Calgary Police Service in Oct 2025 to Sep 2026, against 64 in the same months a year before. Per resident that is 9.7 crimes per 1,000 people a year; Calgary as a whole has 16.3. That is the 148th highest rate of 209 lived-in communities. Counts are the police’s, by community and month; the police do not publish where each crime happened.

Growth and new homes

11 new homes were finished and 10 were approved for building in Nov 2025 to Oct 2026; 16 development permits were issued. The community has about 1,474 homes and had 1,510 households at the 2021 Census.

Where the figures come from

311 requests: The City of Calgary, 311 Service Requests open data (data.calgary.ca, iahh-g8bj), the last 12 complete months against the 12 before. Crime: Calgary Police Service, community crime statistics, the latest 12 months the police have published. Residents: City's total, shared by actual growth, with the 2021 Census count beside it. New homes and permits: City of Calgary building and development permit open data. Rates per 1,000 residents a year use residents in the middle of each period.
