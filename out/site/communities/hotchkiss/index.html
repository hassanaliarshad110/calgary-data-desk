Hotchkiss, Calgary

Hotchkiss is a community in Ward 12 of Calgary, home to about 1,899 people. Residents and businesses made 1,086 requests to 311 in Nov 2025 to Oct 2026, up 28.8% on the year before. 49 requests are open now, 26 of them for more than 90 days. The Calgary Police Service recorded 28 crimes here in Oct 2025 to Sep 2026 (18.9 per 1,000 residents a year; Calgary as a whole: 16.3), up 27.3% on the year before.

Crime in Hotchkiss

28 crimes were reported to the Calgary Police Service in Oct 2025 to Sep 2026, against 22 in the same months a year before. Per resident that is 18.9 crimes per 1,000 people a year; Calgary as a whole has 16.3. That is the 67th highest rate of 209 lived-in communities. Counts are the police’s, by community and month; the police do not publish where each crime happened.

Growth and new homes

397 new homes were finished and 255 were approved for building in Nov 2025 to Oct 2026; 13 development permits were issued.

Where the figures come from

311 requests: The City of Calgary, 311 Service Requests open data (data.calgary.ca, iahh-g8bj), the last 12 complete months against the 12 before. Crime: Calgary Police Service, community crime statistics, the latest 12 months the police have published. Residents: City's total, shared by actual growth, with the 2021 Census count beside it. New homes and permits: City of Calgary building and development permit open data. Rates per 1,000 residents a year use residents in the middle of each period.
