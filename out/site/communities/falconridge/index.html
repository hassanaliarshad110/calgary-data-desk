Falconridge, Calgary

Falconridge is a community in Ward 5 of Calgary, home to about 11,369 people (10,325 at the 2021 Census, up 10.1% since). Residents and businesses made 4,159 requests to 311 in Nov 2025 to Oct 2026, up 4.5% on the year before. 454 requests are open now, 333 of them for more than 90 days. The Calgary Police Service recorded 197 crimes here in Oct 2025 to Sep 2026 (17.3 per 1,000 residents a year; Calgary as a whole: 16.3), up 5.9% on the year before.

Crime in Falconridge

197 crimes were reported to the Calgary Police Service in Oct 2025 to Sep 2026, against 186 in the same months a year before. Per resident that is 17.3 crimes per 1,000 people a year; Calgary as a whole has 16.3. That is the 80th highest rate of 209 lived-in communities. Counts are the police’s, by community and month; the police do not publish where each crime happened.

Growth and new homes

18 new homes were finished and 15 were approved for building in Nov 2025 to Oct 2026; 18 development permits were issued. The community has about 3,113 homes and had 3,220 households at the 2021 Census.

Where the figures come from

311 requests: The City of Calgary, 311 Service Requests open data (data.calgary.ca, iahh-g8bj), the last 12 complete months against the 12 before. Crime: Calgary Police Service, community crime statistics, the latest 12 months the police have published. Residents: City's total, shared by actual growth, with the 2021 Census count beside it. New homes and permits: City of Calgary building and development permit open data. Rates per 1,000 residents a year use residents in the middle of each period.
