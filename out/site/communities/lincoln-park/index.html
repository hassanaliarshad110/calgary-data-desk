Lincoln Park, Calgary

Lincoln Park is a community in Ward 8 of Calgary, home to about 1,749 people (1,590 at the 2021 Census, up 10.0% since). Residents and businesses made 556 requests to 311 in Nov 2025 to Oct 2026, down 9.4% on the year before. 121 requests are open now, 79 of them for more than 90 days. The Calgary Police Service recorded 62 crimes here in Oct 2025 to Sep 2026 (35.4 per 1,000 residents a year; Calgary as a whole: 16.3), up 6.9% on the year before.

Crime in Lincoln Park

62 crimes were reported to the Calgary Police Service in Oct 2025 to Sep 2026, against 58 in the same months a year before. Per resident that is 35.4 crimes per 1,000 people a year; Calgary as a whole has 16.3. That is the 14th highest rate of 209 lived-in communities. Counts are the police’s, by community and month; the police do not publish where each crime happened.

Growth and new homes

0 new homes were finished and 0 were approved for building in Nov 2025 to Oct 2026; 7 development permits were issued. The community has about 971 homes and had 925 households at the 2021 Census.

Where the figures come from

311 requests: The City of Calgary, 311 Service Requests open data (data.calgary.ca, iahh-g8bj), the last 12 complete months against the 12 before. Crime: Calgary Police Service, community crime statistics, the latest 12 months the police have published. Residents: City's total, shared by actual growth, with the 2021 Census count beside it. New homes and permits: City of Calgary building and development permit open data. Rates per 1,000 residents a year use residents in the middle of each period.
