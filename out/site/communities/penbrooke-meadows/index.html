Penbrooke Meadows, Calgary

Penbrooke Meadows is a community in Ward 9 of Calgary, home to about 9,068 people (8,235 at the 2021 Census, up 10.1% since). Residents and businesses made 2,091 requests to 311 in Nov 2025 to Oct 2026, down 6.0% on the year before. 252 requests are open now, 219 of them for more than 90 days. The Calgary Police Service recorded 124 crimes here in Oct 2025 to Sep 2026 (13.7 per 1,000 residents a year; Calgary as a whole: 16.3), down 10.8% on the year before.

Crime in Penbrooke Meadows

124 crimes were reported to the Calgary Police Service in Oct 2025 to Sep 2026, against 139 in the same months a year before. Per resident that is 13.7 crimes per 1,000 people a year; Calgary as a whole has 16.3. That is the 106th highest rate of 209 lived-in communities. Counts are the police’s, by community and month; the police do not publish where each crime happened.

Growth and new homes

16 new homes were finished and 13 were approved for building in Nov 2025 to Oct 2026; 17 development permits were issued. The community has about 2,793 homes and had 3,005 households at the 2021 Census.

Where the figures come from
