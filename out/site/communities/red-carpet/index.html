Red Carpet, Calgary

Red Carpet is a community in Ward 9 of Calgary, home to about 1,922 people (1,745 at the 2021 Census, up 10.1% since). Residents and businesses made 300 requests to 311 in Nov 2025 to Oct 2026, down 20.2% on the year before. 41 requests are open now, 38 of them for more than 90 days. The Calgary Police Service recorded 52 crimes here in Oct 2025 to Sep 2026 (27.0 per 1,000 residents a year; Calgary as a whole: 16.3), up 18.2% on the year before.

Where the figures come from

Crime: Calgary Police Service, community crime statistics, the latest 12 months the police have published. Residents: City's total, shared by actual growth, with the 2021 Census count beside it.
