Hounsfield Heights/Briar Hill, Calgary

Hounsfield Heights/Briar Hill is a community in Ward 7 of Calgary, home to about 2,820 people (2,470 at the 2021 Census, up 14.2% since). Residents and businesses made 1,651 requests to 311 in Nov 2025 to Oct 2026, down 0.1% on the year before. 283 requests are open now, 235 of them for more than 90 days. The Calgary Police Service recorded 129 crimes here in Oct 2025 to Sep 2026 (46.0 per 1,000 residents a year; Calgary as a whole: 16.3), up 17.3% on the year before.

Crime in Hounsfield Heights/Briar Hill

129 crimes were reported to the Calgary Police Service in Oct 2025 to Sep 2026, against 110 in the same months a year before. Per resident that is 46.0 crimes per 1,000 people a year; Calgary as a whole has 16.3. That is the 9th highest rate of 209 lived-in communities. Counts are the police’s, by community and month; the police do not publish where each crime happened.

Growth and new homes

27 new homes were finished and 17 were approved for building in Nov 2025 to Oct 2026; 31 development permits were issued. The community has about 1,068 homes and had 1,040 households at the 2021 Census.
