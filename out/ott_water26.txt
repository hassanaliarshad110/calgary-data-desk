Sign up today for Water Usage Alerts
You can sign up through My ServiceOttawa to receive water usage alerts when:
• Your water consumption exceeds your preset threshold
• You have continuous usage over seven consecutive days
• Your consumption exceeds your average monthly use
You can also view monthly, daily and hourly consumption and compare monthly usage. These tools help property
owners better manage their consumption and monitor usage in the fastest and most efficient way possible. Residents
can also sign up to receive paperless billing.

The City maintains drinking water, wastewater and stormwater assets, including
• Over 6,000 km of sanitary and stormwater collection pipes
• Over 3,000 km of watermains
• 6,600 small and medium culverts
• Over 270 stormwater management facilities
• Two water purification plants - Britannia and Lemieux Island
• One wastewater treatment facility - The Robert O. Pickard Environmental Centre (ROPEC)
• 55 wastewater pumping stations
Investing in our drinking water, wastewater and stormwater systems
• $23.5 million to ensure that stormwater infrastructure is safely managed and conveyed
• $36.5 million to replace and repair drainage culverts
• $51.6 million for drinking water infrastructure
• $11.3 million for drinking water treatment, to ensure a continued supply of clean, safe drinking water
• $50.6 million for wastewater infrastructure
• $87.2 million for wastewater treatment, to ensure that wastewater is safely conveyed and treated in a way that
protects the environment.
