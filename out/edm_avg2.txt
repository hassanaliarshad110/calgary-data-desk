HOW DO YOU COMPARE?
EXPLAINING THE RELATIONSHIP BETWEEN THE CHANGE
IN YOUR ASSESSED VALUE AND PROPERTY TAXES
The City of Edmonton uses the assessed value of your property to calculate your property taxes. However, increases or
decreases in assessed values do not automatically translate into increases or decreases in property taxes.
To anticipate the property tax change you might see on your next tax bill, it's critically important to understand how your property's
assessed value change compares to the overall assessed property value change in Edmonton. The examples below feature
residential assessment and tax impacts.
