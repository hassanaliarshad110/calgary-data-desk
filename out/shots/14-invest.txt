How this is measured. From every home on the City's property roll (weighted by its units) to the businesses of each kind around it, wherever they are: a supermarket 400 m away across the community line counts. Walking distance asks: is there one close by? A gap: under 60% of Calgary's share of homes within 800 m, and at least 1,000 residents without one; only kinds of business that at least a quarter of Calgary's homes have this close are judged. Every community is judged the same way, whatever its size.
