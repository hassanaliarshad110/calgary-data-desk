"Still open" is how the City's data marks them, not proof the work is undone. Many older requests are still marked open, some for years: some may be finished but never closed, especially "311 Contact Us" messages, which the list below leaves out unless you untick the box. The office can send 311 a list of request numbers to review: filter the list, then use "Save this list".
Track a request

Type a 311 request number to see which department has it, its status and its dates

Look up

What a status cannot tell you: "Closed" means the department closed the request in its system. It does not confirm the problem was fixed: the work may be scheduled for later, the request may have been passed to another City system or team, or it may be outside the City's responsibility. The open data has no notes on what was done, who did it, or when the department took it on; it shows the department responsible, the status and the dates. Request numbers name no one. Source: City of Calgary 311 Service Requests (iahh-g8bj), published daily.
