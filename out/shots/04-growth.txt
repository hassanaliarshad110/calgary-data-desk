Are new homes keeping up?

Homes built since 2021 are about as near as older homes to playgrounds (68% within 400 m, against 72%) and parks (97% within 400 m, against 99%), but farther from transit stops (70% within 400 m, against 97%), community centres (18% within 800 m, against 41%), indoor recreation centres (30% within 2 km, against 52%) and libraries (33% within 2 km, against 53%).

Crowding at playgrounds and parks
ESTIMATE
Playgrounds within 400 m: 4.08 playgrounds per 1,000 children across Calgary; 2.71 in the 31 communities where a quarter or more of homes were built since 2021, 4.33 in the rest.
Parks within 400 m: 5.9 park hectares per 1,000 residents across Calgary; 4.9 in the 31 communities where a quarter or more of homes were built since 2021, 6.0 in the rest.
Near, but crowded (playgrounds): 9 communities in Ward 5 have most homes within 400 m of a playground, yet what is within reach is shared by more children than Calgary's average (score under 0.80; 8 of them under every assumption). Examples: Cityscape 0.28, Falconridge 0.38, Skyview Ranch 0.39, Redstone 0.39, Homestead 0.44. The typical community scores 0.44 for this amenity: Calgary's figure is an average over every resident and is lifted by the communities next to a few very large parks, so 9 of 10 communities fall under 0.80.
