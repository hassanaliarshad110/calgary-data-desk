THE GAME
Calgary, built from the City's own open data

YYC City Stories is an open-world game set on the real map of Calgary. Every street, building and park is where it is in real life, built from the City of Calgary's open data. You walk, drive, take the bus and ride the CTrain through the city as one of five characters, and every mission teaches a real City service: what it is, where to find it, and how to use it.

It was made for newcomers finding their feet, students, seniors and anyone who lives here, to show that help is closer than people think.

WHAT MAKES IT REAL
Not a city like Calgary. Calgary.
🏙️
Every building, its real shape

Building shapes come from the City's 3D models made from LiDAR, and the ground follows the City's real elevation model, hills, river valleys and all.

🏠
House numbers on every home

Every building carries its real address from the City's Parcel Address data. Type an address in the in-game phone and go there.

🏪
Real shops and businesses

Storefront signs on the right buildings, from the City's business licences. The phone's Nearby app finds them around you.

🚦
Real streets, signals and signs

Traffic lights stand at the City's real signalised intersections, with speed limits by street type and road signs from City data.

🚆
Calgary Transit

Buses on their real routes and CTrains on both lines, with real stops and shelters from the Calgary Transit timetable. Pay the real fare ($4.00 adult, good for 90 minutes) and ride.

🧭
Directions by transit

Pick a place on the map and follow the GPS route, or tap "Guide me by transit" for step-by-step directions: walk to this stop, take this bus, change here.

🌳
Parks as they are

Benches, picnic tables, playgrounds and fountains each at their real spot, from City of Calgary Parks open data.

🏏
Real sports fields

More than 1,300 soccer, football, rugby and cricket fields from the City's sports field data, with their lines, goals, posts, boundary ropes and cricket pitches, and on the map.

🤠
It's Stampede time

Stampede Park is in full swing: a midway with a Ferris wheel and rides, the Grandstand Show with fireworks every night, a country stage, crowds in cowboy hats and a street party at Olympic Plaza.

🗳️
Your ward and councillor

Welcome billboards where main roads enter each of the 14 wards, with a welcome from that ward's councillor.

📱
A phone with real City info

311, Transit, Find help and City facts, including the number of 311 requests open across Calgary right now, live from open data.

🎤
Day, night and concerts

The city lights up after dark. Walk up to a stage to hear the title song, "Under One Sky", with the lyrics on screen to sing along.

🗣️
Voiced stories

Story dialogue is spoken in all six languages, and Natalia's story ends with a music video, "Beyond the Mask".

🚗
Walk, drive or fly

Get in a car, hop on a motorbike or fly the helicopter over downtown and the Bow.

📷
See it for real

At mission places and landmarks, open the real street photo of the spot and compare it with the game.

🌐
Six languages

English, French, Punjabi, Urdu, Tagalog and Arabic: every mission, card and quiz.

CHOOSE WHO YOU PLAY
Five characters, five stories

Each character has their own story, set in the parts of the city they live in. Every mission ends with a short card on the real service, with links to the official page, and a quick quiz.
