What a status cannot tell you: "Closed" means the department closed the request in its system. It does not confirm the problem was fixed: the work may be scheduled for later, the request may have been passed to another City system or team, or it may be outside the City's responsibility. The open data has no notes on what was done, who did it, or when the department took it on; it shows the department responsible, the status and the dates. Request numbers name no one. Days to close = the closed date minus the date the request was made; the average counts closed requests only. Requests made near the end of the period have had less time to close, so "still open" is higher for recent work. What a status cannot tell you: "Closed" means the department closed the request in its system. It does not confirm the problem was fixed: the work may be scheduled for later, the request may have been passed to another City system or team, or it may be outside the City's responsibility. The open data has no notes on what was done, who did it, or when the department took it on; it shows the department responsible, the status and the dates.

Request details come from the City's 311 open data, updated daily. The City publishes the community for each request, not its address, and its own status page shows a request only to the person who filed it.
