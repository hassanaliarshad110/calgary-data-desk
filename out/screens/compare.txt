Side by side

Two different comparisons. Under each ward's figure: its place among the 14 wards (for example "2nd lowest of 14 wards": a place, not a judgment; fewer 311 reports can mean problems are not being reported). "Against Calgary": how far it is from Calgary's own figure, which is all 14 wards added together: every resident and every request placed in a community, so a big ward counts for more than a small one. A ward can be 2nd lowest of 14 and 32% below Calgary at the same time: they measure different things. Green or red only where higher or lower is clearly better (crime, traffic incidents, requests closed within 7 days, amenities), and only when 10% or more from Calgary.
