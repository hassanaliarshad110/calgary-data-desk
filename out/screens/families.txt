What a rule breach means: a provincial licensing officer inspected the program and found something that did not meet Alberta's child care licensing rules, for example staff-to-child ratios, records, equipment or safety. The usual result is an "order to remedy": the program must fix it by a set date, and a follow-up visit checks it. A breach does not mean a child was harmed; many are paperwork or equipment. Licensed spaces: how many children each licensed program may care for at once, for programs whose address is in Calgary (Government of Alberta, Child Care Information). Family day home agencies are listed but not counted, because the day homes they oversee are spread across the city. 23 programs (1,957 spaces) have a Calgary address the City's address list could not place: they are in Calgary's total but in no community or ward. "Licensed since" is the first quarter the program appears in the province's files (from March 2021). Source: Government of Alberta, Child Care Information.
