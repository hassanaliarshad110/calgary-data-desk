What each layer is. News stories: Calgary News articles placed where the story happened, as its sources describe it; each popup gives the key facts and links to the original reports. Traffic: incidents the City of Calgary reports (dataset 35ra-9556), read again every 2 minutes while this page is open. 311 requests: requests for City service (potholes, noise, graffiti), not crimes; the City's file is updated daily (dataset iahh-g8bj). Crime by community: crimes reported to the Calgary Police Service per 1,000 residents a year, Oct 2025 to Sep 2026. The police publish counts by community and month, not the place of each crime, so single crimes are not on the map.

If you see something happening, or have information about a crime

911
Emergency: a crime in progress, a fire, or someone hurt or in danger
403-266-1234
Calgary Police non-emergency: report a crime after the fact or something suspicious
1-800-222-8477
Crime Stoppers: give a tip anonymously (calgarycrimestoppers.org)
311
City services: potholes, graffiti, noise, an abandoned vehicle (403-268-2489 from outside Calgary)
511
Alberta 511: highway and road conditions
