Notes on these figures
Growing: more than 30% more residents than at the 2021 Census, or built since then.
Residents are counted with City's total, shared by actual growth (chosen in Settings): All of Calgary adds up to the City's own population estimate and forecast for that month, but growth is not spread evenly: each community is its 2021 Census count times one factor, plus the growth the growth model measured there (homes built since 2021). The factor spreads only what homes do not explain (more people per home since 2021, people in seniors' homes, residences and shelters, the Census undercount) over every community's 2021 count. Fast-building areas get their real growth; built-out ones grow less. The 2021 Census count is shown beside it. Rates per 1,000 residents a year use residents in the middle of each period.
Infrastructure reports are the 21 roads, water and sewer, and parks and trees service types used in the 311 equity study.
The City publishes most 311 requests at their community's centre point, not their address, so 311 is mapped by community, not by street.
Traffic incidents are the City's reported incidents with exact locations, inside the City boundary.
Crime and disorder by community come from the Calgary Police Service community safety data (the data behind its Community Safety Dashboard), published every quarter by month. Incidents without a location are left out. The Crime Severity Index (Statistics Canada) compares Calgary with other cities each year.
