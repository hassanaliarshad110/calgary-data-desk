If every year passes as proposed, the City would collect about 44% more property tax from existing properties in 2030 than in 2026 (each year's increase compounds on the last). 2027: The City of Calgary (calgary.ca/budget). 2028 to 2030: news reports of the proposed budget presented to Council on November 10, 2026 (LiveWire Calgary, Yahoo News Canada); the City's budget page gives only the 2027 figure, so treat the later years as reported, not confirmed. The City says each 1% of property tax is about $25.6 million; the 2027 increase would be the largest since 1996 (LiveWire Calgary). Calgary's five-year average increase was 3.28%, second lowest among large Canadian municipalities (LiveWire Calgary, citing the budget).

City share is the municipal tax the City keeps; the rest of each bill is the Province's education tax, which the City collects and passes to the Government of Alberta. City projects by ward

The City publishes its budget by department and service, not by ward. What can be placed in each ward is City of Calgary projects being built or planned, and City road projects, each with the whole project's published cost. That is usually spent over several years and often shared with the province or the federal government, so it is not a year's budget and is not divided per resident. Private and other-government projects are on Growth vs amenities.
