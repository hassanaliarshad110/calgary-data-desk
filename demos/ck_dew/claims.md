Not claimed: that chasing always wins. Wisden's analysis of IPL night games (2022–2024) found results close to 50-50, so the script says only why captains often choose to chase and gives Dubai 2021 as an example ("misaal"), not as proof. Three of the 13 Dubai matches were afternoon games, so the screen adds the night-match figure. Steyn's soap line is a paraphrase of "now you are trying to do that with this wet bar of soap" and is not shown in quotation marks.
