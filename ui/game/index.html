The game

Calgary, built from the City's own open data

YYC City Stories is an open-world game set on the real map of Calgary. Every street, building and park is where it is in real life, built from the City of Calgary's open data. You walk, drive and ride the CTrain through the city as one of four Calgarians, and every mission teaches a real City service: what it is, where to find it, and how to use it.

It was made for newcomers finding their feet, students, seniors and anyone who lives here, to show that help is closer than people think.

What makes it real

Not a city like Calgary. Calgary.

🏙️Every building, its real shape

Building shapes come from the City's 3D models made from LiDAR, and the ground follows the City's real elevation model, hills, river valleys and all.

🏠House numbers on every home

Every building carries its real address from the City's Parcel Address data. Type an address in the in-game phone and go there.

🏪Real shops and businesses

Storefront signs on the right buildings, from the City's business licences. The phone's Nearby app finds them around you.

🚦Real streets, signals and signs

Traffic lights stand at the City's real signalised intersections, with speed limits by street type and road signs from City data.

🚆Calgary Transit

Real routes and stops from the Calgary Transit timetable. Walk onto any CTrain platform, pick your stop and ride.

🌳Parks as they are

Benches, picnic tables, playgrounds and fountains each at their real spot, from City of Calgary Parks open data.

🗳️Your ward and councillor

Welcome billboards where main roads enter each of the 14 wards, with a welcome from that ward's councillor.

📱A phone with real City info

311, Transit, Find help and City facts, including the number of 311 requests open across Calgary right now, live from open data.

🎤Day, night and a concert

The city lights up after dark, and a concert plays at the Scotiabank Saddledome every night to the title song, "Under One Sky".

🚗Walk, drive or fly

Get in a car, hop on a motorbike or fly the helicopter over downtown and the Bow.

📷See it for real

At mission places and landmarks, open the real street photo of the spot and compare it with the game.

🌐Six languages

English, French, Punjabi, Urdu, Tagalog and Arabic: every mission, card and quiz.

How to play

Free, in your browser

On a computer

  • W A S D to move, drag the mouse to look around
  • Shift to run or boost, F to get in or out of a car
  • E to talk or use, M for the map, P for your phone

On a phone or tablet

  • On-screen joystick to move, drag to look around
  • Buttons for the phone, map, car and helicopter
  • Turn the phone sideways for the best view; a recent phone works best

Two ways to play

  • Story: follow your character's missions in order
  • Free roam: explore anywhere in Calgary, ride the CTrain, find landmarks
  • Your progress is saved in your browser
