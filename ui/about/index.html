Why Calgary Insight exists

Calgary Insight puts the City of Calgary's public data in one place, ward by ward and community by community, in plain words, for anyone who lives here.

Built with passion and love for the city

Calgary Insight is built by one person who lives here and loves this city. It is a labour of love, made to give something back to Calgary.

The City, the police, the Province and other public bodies publish a great deal of information, but it is spread across hundreds of datasets and reports. It is brought together and explained here, so that residents, community associations, newcomers, businesses and elected offices can all look at the same facts about their own community.

What you will find

  • Every ward and community: residents and growth, 311 requests, safety, traffic and new homes. Each has its own page: Calgary's 14 wards and every Calgary community.
  • Growth against amenities: how near homes are to parks, playgrounds, transit, libraries and recreation centres, and how crowded they are, today and as the city grows.
  • What is being built in every ward, the City budget, elections and who represents you.
  • Data Genie: ask a question in plain words and get an answer from the official data, with its sources.

How it works

  • Independent and non-partisan. Calgary Insight is not part of, or funded by, the City of Calgary, any political party, candidate or advocacy group. Every ward, community and candidate is treated the same way.
  • Official sources. Every figure comes from published public data and names its source, so you can check it.
  • Refreshed every morning. The data is read again each day; the schedule is on the Data sources page.
  • Explained. How every number is counted is written out on the "How it's counted" page.
  • Open to everyone. No account, no paywall, no tracking of who you are.
