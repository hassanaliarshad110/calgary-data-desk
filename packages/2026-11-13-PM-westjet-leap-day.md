## 1. Five potential topics / angles (researched this run)
1. The leap day: WestJet first began flying on Feb. 29, 1996 (Global News; CTV Calgary: "didn't make its first flight until February 29th").
2. The founders: the Calgary-based airline was founded by Clive Beddoe and a team of partners (Global News).
3. The start: just three aircraft (Global News).
4. The first map: five destinations, Vancouver, Kelowna, Edmonton, Calgary and Winnipeg (Global News).
5. Opened for business February 5, 1996, weeks before the first flight (CTV Calgary). Not used (keeps one idea).
Numbers left out on purpose: staff then and now, and today's destination count (they date quickly and the sources give different "now" figures).
