# Who was Crowchild? The chief behind Crowchild Trail
Date/slot: 2026-11-11 PM · Type: quick-insight (fun fact) · Skill: calgary-data-explainer · Category: Fun fact
Calendar topic: "Crowchild Trail is named after Tsuut'ina Chief David Crowchild" (kept; verified this run in Calgary Guardian, Avenue Calgary, The Sprawl, mycalgary.com / Imagine, Alberta Champions and Windspeaker/AMMSA). Not in log.json or on the covered list. Related in format to the Nov 10 PM Deerfoot video (road-name series), but a different person and story.
Format: 1080×1920 vertical, 15–35 s, Kokoro `af_heart`, free motion-graphics render.

## 1. Five potential topics / angles (researched this run)
1. The blessing: city officials asked David Crowchild's permission to use his name, and he gave his blessing (The Sprawl, Dec 8, 2018).
2. His words on the honour: "May this be a symbol of cutting all barriers between all peoples for all times to come" (Alberta Champions; mycalgary.com / Imagine, Mar 2023).
3. Who he was: chief of the Tsuut'ina (then Sarcee) Nation, 1946–1953 (Calgary Guardian; Avenue Calgary).
4. A bridge builder: worked to build understanding between Indigenous and non-Indigenous cultures (The Sprawl); a Calgary award, the Chief David Crowchild Memorial Award, honours people who bridge that gap (Windspeaker 1987).
5. Naming date: sources disagree (1966 in The Sprawl; 1971 in Imagine; Windspeaker says he cut the ribbon in 1968). Not used.

## 2. Recommendation
Angles 3 + 1 + 2 as one mini-story: name → who → the ask → his words. It has a hook (a road everyone drives), a surprise (they asked him first) and an ending (his quote). No dates for the naming are used because the sources disagree.
