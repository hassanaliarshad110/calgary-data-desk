## 3. Why this story matters
Most Calgarians know Bowness, Forest Lawn, Montgomery and Mission as neighbourhoods. Few know they were separate places with their own councils, and that the city's map was drawn by a string of annexations, some welcomed and some resisted. Annexation is still how Calgary adds land (the City's annexation page calls it a primary means of growth), so the story explains why the map looks the way it does today, without taking a side on how the city should grow next.
