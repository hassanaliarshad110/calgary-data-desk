## 3. Why this story matters
Every Calgarian says the name every day, but few know it was a fight: an officer named the new fort after himself, the Mounted Police cancelled it, and a colonel put forward a place on a Scottish island. The letter that settled it survives in the City's archives, and the name it chose is 150 years old this year. It also corrects two common beliefs: that "Calgary" means "clear running water", and that cleared Mull crofters brought the name with them.
