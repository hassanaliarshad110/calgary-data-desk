## 3. Why it matters
Calgary is Canada's energy head-office city. Most people date that to Leduc in 1947. The habit started 33 years earlier, in a speculative frenzy over a Turner Valley well that produced wet gas and naphtha, not the crude investors dreamed of. The story explains the city's boom-bust character in miniature: a discovery, a rush, a war, a bust, and still the head offices stayed.
