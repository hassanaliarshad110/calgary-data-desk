## 1. What happened / why it matters / what happens next
- **What happened:** on Thursday, Nov 26, 2026, the City of Calgary said the new Bearspaw South Feeder Main "is connected to our water system and is now ready for commissioning" (City of Calgary, Stage 4 water restrictions page, Nov 26 update; also City newsroom, LiveWire Calgary and CJWE, Nov 26, as summarised in our Calgary News article of Nov 26).
- **Why it matters:** city-wide Stage 4 outdoor water restrictions have been in effect since Nov 4 while the pipe was connected; until the new pipe is in service, the City says there is still a risk of a break.
- **What happens next:** refilling of the existing pipe is expected to begin Nov 27, followed by water-quality testing and system checks; "If all goes as planned, we expect to lift water restrictions later next week." The new pipe must be pressure tested, cleaned, flushed and refilled and pass water-quality testing before becoming operational later in December.
- **Why this story:** the most widely felt Calgary story of the past 48 hours (every household is under Stage 4); official source plus City newsroom, LiveWire Calgary and CJWE reports (Nov 26), and our own Calgary News article (Nov 26, 4 sources). New since our Nov 9 flagship "The Pipe Under the Bow" (which covered the breaks, the review panel and the start of connection work): the new main is now connected, the refill and lift timeline is new. Passed over: budget deliberations (covered Nov 25), the provincial fiscal update (not Calgary-specific), a theft charge (court matter).
