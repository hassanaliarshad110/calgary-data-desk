## 1. What happened / why it matters / what happens next
- **What happened:** On Tuesday, Nov 17, 2026, Calgary city council voted 13-2 for a motion to explore a land-use bylaw amendment that could allow a temporary pause on new large ("hyperscale or mega-scale") data centres. Two councillors voted against.
- **Why it matters:** There is no pause yet. The City's Land Use Bylaw has to be amended first, and that requires a public hearing. Supporters point to limits on electricity and water; an industry group says a pause would send the wrong signal to investors. One large data centre application is already under review in the far northeast, near Redstone.
- **What happens next:** Administration brings proposed bylaw changes by March 2027; the public hearing is expected in the spring; a report on the risks, implications and opportunities of data centres is due by the end of 2027.

**Why this story:** the most-talked-about City Hall decision of the past 48 hours (Calgary Herald, Global News, LiveWire Calgary, CTV), with a "does this affect me" angle (power, water, the northeast). The snowfall is being covered by the same-day moments watch (log 2026-11-17 MOMENT and 2026-11-18 MOMENT watch), so it is not repeated here. Not in log.json. We published it in Calgary News on Nov 17.
