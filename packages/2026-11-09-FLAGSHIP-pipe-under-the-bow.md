## 2. Three alternative opening hooks

2. "The warning came in two thousand four. The failure came in twenty twenty-four."

## 3. Why this story matters
- One 1975 concrete pipe carries up to about 60% of the city's potable water (Independent Review Panel). When it fails, the whole city changes how it uses water.
- It failed in June 2024 and again on December 30, 2025, and Calgarians are under Stage 4 restrictions again (from November 4, 2026) while the replacement is connected.
- The independent review shows the risk was known from internal assessments after the 2004 McKnight feeder main failure, and that inspections designated in 2017, 2020 and 2022 did not occur. That is the under-reported part: this was not a surprise failure.
- Most viewers know "the water main broke". Few know the replacement is microtunnelled under the Bow River, or that more of the old line (Parkdale Boulevard) is still being repaired.

## Methodology and limitations
- "Up to about sixty per cent" is the Panel's wording ("up to approximately 60%"); it is a capacity share, not a constant daily share.
- "Drinking water" is used for the Panel's "potable water".
- The $38.2M covers the initial break and 29 additional segments (City FAQ). The FAQ's own table shows "2 planned urgent repairs" for Oct 16 to Nov 26, 2024 while the text says three segments in October; the narration uses only the FAQ's total (29) and cost, not the monthly split.
- The Independent Review was completed before the December 30, 2025 break and does not cover it.
- Schedule items (four weeks, end of 2026, early December) are City targets as of Oct 28, 2026; as of Nov 9 the City said the weekend valve setback was not expected to delay the overall schedule.
- The route schematic is not to scale. It shows the Shaganappi Pump Station (near Edworthy Park) on one bank of the Bow and 73 Street N.W. / Bowness on the other, and lists the Stage A crossings as the City does ("16 Avenue, the Bow River and the CPKC rail line") without drawing their order, which the City page does not give.
- The City PDF of the Final Report could not be opened in this run (permission timeout); the identical report hosted by LiveWire Calgary was read.
