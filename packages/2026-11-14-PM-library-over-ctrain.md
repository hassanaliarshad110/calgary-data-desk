## 1. Five potential topics / angles (researched this run)
1. The library was built over an active CTrain line, a Calgary first (CMLC, Sep 2015).
2. 150 metres of track encased in concrete, with only nine weekend shutdowns in 18 months (CMLC, Sep 2015).
3. The tracks arc across the site and dip from street level into a tunnel next to City Hall station (CMLC, May 2014).
4. The wooden entrance arch is western red cedar, shaped after the Chinook arch clouds (The Architect's Newspaper, Nov 2018).
5. The building's form was shaped by the curving LRT line (Canadian Consulting Engineer, Dec 2014).

## Methodology and limitations
