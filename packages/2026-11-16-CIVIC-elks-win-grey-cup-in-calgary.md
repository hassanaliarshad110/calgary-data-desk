## 1. What happened / why it matters / what happens next
- **What happened:** the 113th Grey Cup was played Sunday night, Nov 15, 2026, at McMahon Stadium in Calgary. The Edmonton Elks beat the Montreal Alouettes 37-30. Quarterback Cody Fajardo was named Grey Cup MVP.
- **Why it matters:** the most-talked-about Calgary story of the past 48 hours: the city hosted the CFL championship for the sixth time, the first since 2019. For the Elks it is their first Grey Cup since 2015 and the franchise's fifteenth.
- **What happens next:** the 114th Grey Cup goes to Regina in 2027, and from 2027 the CFL plays on a shorter field.
- Why this story: biggest Calgary story since Saturday (national coverage, game in our city). The Nov 13 video was a preview; the result is new. The budget was covered Nov 9, 11 and in the Nov 14-15 film; water restrictions on Nov 9-10.
