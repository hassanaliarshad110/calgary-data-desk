## 3. Why this story matters
Every Calgarian writes NW, NE, SW or SE at the end of their address without thinking about it. The numbers replaced a set of railway-era names in 1904, people kept using the old names for years, and one of them, Stephen Avenue, came back on the pedestrian mall in 1981. The same grid still decides where an address is, and its quirks (a centre point on a bridge, avenues that follow the railway rather than true east-west) explain why newcomers get lost.
