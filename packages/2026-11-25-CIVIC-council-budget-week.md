## 1. What happened / why it matters / what happens next
- **What happened:** on Monday, Nov 23, 2026, Calgary city council began deliberating the proposed 2027-2030 budget with a public hearing; 264 speakers had registered by Monday afternoon (Calgary Herald). A councillor (Ward 1 Coun. Kim Tyers) asked administration for a zero per cent scenario (LiveWire Calgary). The mayor called the Calgary Police Service capital request "unprecedented"; the Calgary Police Commission chair said "There is no room to cut in this police budget" (CBC News).
- **Why it matters:** the proposal requires a 15 per cent increase in municipal property tax revenue from existing properties in 2027; for a typical single residential property the City estimates about $37 more a month in municipal property tax (City of Calgary, Nov 10, 2026).
- **What happens next:** councillors had submitted at least 69 budget amendments as of Monday morning (Calgary Herald); the City says final approval follows the debate (City of Calgary).
- **Why this story:** the biggest and most-talked-about Calgary story of the past 48 hours (CBC, CTV, Calgary Herald, LiveWire Calgary, Calgary Citizen; two Calgary News articles on our site, Nov 23 and Nov 24). New since our Nov 9 and Nov 11 budget videos (preliminary and proposed numbers): council's deliberations and public hearing have begun, the zero per cent request, the police-budget debate and the amendments. Passed over: water restrictions (covered recently), three officers charged (court matter), Bill 43 memo (provincial bill; our Nov 20 video blocked).
