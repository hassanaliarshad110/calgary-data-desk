## 3. Why this story matters
Every Calgarian has lived a chinook: the arch over the mountains, the sudden thaw, the slush, the headache some people swear by. Few know how it works, where its name comes from, or that it once warmed the city thirty degrees in an afternoon. It shapes how the city lives through winter: snow, trees, roads and moods.
