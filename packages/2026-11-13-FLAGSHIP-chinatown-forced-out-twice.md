## 3. Why this story matters
Chinatown is one of downtown's most familiar places, but few Calgarians know it sits on its third site, or that the community bought its own land in 1910 after being pushed out twice, and later fought off a road that would have cut it in half. It is a story of displacement, persistence and what a neighbourhood has to do to stay put.
