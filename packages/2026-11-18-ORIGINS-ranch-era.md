Slot ORIGINS (weekly documentary) · skills: calgary-origins-documentary + calgary-motion-director (lead identity **Storyteller**, borrowing Data Explainer counter slams and branding stamps, Civic Analyst document cards and timelines) · formats: 1920×1080 film (four chapters) + four 1080×1920 reels “PART n OF 4”.
