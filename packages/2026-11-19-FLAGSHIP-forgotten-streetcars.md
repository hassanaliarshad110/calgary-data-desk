## 3. Why this story matters
Every day Calgarians ride the CTrain along 7 Avenue without knowing the city had already built, run and scrapped an electric street railway. The streetcars decided where early suburbs like South Calgary grew and even helped create Bowness Park; the city then chose buses, and it took more than thirty years for rail to return. The last streetcar still exists, restored, at Heritage Park.
