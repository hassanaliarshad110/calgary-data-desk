## 1. Topics / angles considered (researched this run)
1. A provincial park inside Calgary city limits, one of the largest urban parks in Canada (Alberta Parks; chosen as the hook and fact).
2. The valley's long First Nations history: people arrive 6500 BC, bison pounds used from 2500 BC to the 1800s (Alberta Parks history timeline; used as beat 3).
3. The Bow Valley Ranch: Pat Burns bought it from William Roper Hull in 1902; the province bought it in 1972 (Alberta Parks history page; used as beats 4-5).
4. The park opened in 1975, fifty years ago last year (Alberta Parks; LiveWire Calgary; used).
5. The 1971 decision not to sell the land back to the City helped spur Nose Hill Park (LiveWire Calgary; not used: one idea per video).
