## 1. What happened / why it matters / what happens next
- **What happened:** on Saturday, Nov 21, 2026, Cavalry FC beat Atletico Ottawa 3-2 in the Canadian Premier League (CPL) Final at ATCO Field, Spruce Meadows. Tobias Warschewski scored twice (5th and 74th minutes), in a minus 11 C wind chill, before a capacity crowd of 9,131.
- **Why it matters:** Cavalry had already won the regular season (the CPL Shield). Winning the final makes it the first club in the CPL's eight-year history to win both in the same season. It was Cavalry's fourth straight final and its second title in three years (first in 2024).
- **What happens next:** Cavalry has already qualified for the 2027 Concacaf Champions Cup, which will feature 27 of the region's top clubs.
- **Why this story:** the most-talked-about Calgary story of the past 48 hours (national coverage: TSN/The Canadian Press, CBC, CTV; local: Calgary Journal), a first in league history, played in Calgary. Not in log.json. Other candidates: Bill 43 bike-lane amendments (provincial, details not yet released; our Nov 20 video on it was blocked), budget deliberations (began today; the proposed budget was covered on Nov 11 and outcomes are not yet known).
