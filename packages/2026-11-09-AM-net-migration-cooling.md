## Methodology and limitations
- Boundary: City of Calgary (not the CMA or region). Years are the City's labels; Table 4 gives population "as of April" and net migration "(April-March)".
- 2024 and 2025 are City estimates ("Estimate (no Civic Census)"); 2026 is a forecast completed January 2026. Net migration is total (international + interprovincial + intraprovincial); the source does not split it.
- The City's Fall 2026 outlook is listed on the City page but could not be opened in this run, so any revision it contains is not reflected. The City page states the population outlook is updated in January and published in the Spring outlook.
- "Moved to Calgary than moved away" is the plain-English meaning of net migration (arrivals minus departures).
