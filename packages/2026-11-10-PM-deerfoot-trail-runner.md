# Who was Deerfoot? The runner behind Deerfoot Trail

## 1. Five potential topics / angles (researched this run)
1. The 1886 ten-mile race in Calgary: Deerfoot beat J. W. Stokes of Birmingham, England, by a full lap; Stokes's handlers claimed a miscount; in the rerun Deerfoot entered only after Stokes had completed six laps, still passed him, and finished in 54 min 30 s (DCB).
2. His names: Api-kai-ees means "scabby dried meat"; "Deerfoot" was a professional name used earlier by a Seneca runner who set records at the Crystal Palace in London (DCB).
3. Family: a nephew of Blackfoot head chief Crowfoot (DCB).
4. The four-day "go as you please" endurance race of 1886: 84 miles and 6 laps in the allotted 16 hours (DCB).
5. Remembered: Deerfoot Trail, an industrial centre and a shopping mall named in his memory in the 1970s (DCB); inducted into the Alberta Sports Hall of Fame in 1998 (ASHOF).

## 2. Recommendation
