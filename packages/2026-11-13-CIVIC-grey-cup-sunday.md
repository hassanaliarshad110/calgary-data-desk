Slot: 2026-11-13 CIVIC ("Calgary news explained"), 1080x1920 vertical, target 30-40 s. Lead identity: Civic Analyst (evidence-led), borrowing a Storyteller dusk drone opener and a Data Explainer counter slam. Editorial owner: calgary-storyteller rules + civic neutrality.

## 1. What happened / why it matters / what happens next
- **What happened:** Calgary is hosting the 113th Grey Cup on Sunday, Nov. 15, 2026 at McMahon Stadium: the Montreal Alouettes vs the Edmonton Elks, kickoff 5 p.m. The Grey Cup Festival opened Nov. 11 at Stampede Park. (City of Calgary; CP24/CTV via The Canadian Press; Wikipedia.)
- **Why it matters:** the sixth time Calgary has hosted, the first since 2019; organizers expect roughly $50 million in economic activity (an organizer estimate, CP24).
- **What happens next:** Sunday, 11 a.m. to 11 p.m., University Drive NW closes between 16 and 24 Avenue NW and Crowchild Trail NW drops to two lanes each way; the City says take the Red Line CTrain to Banff Trail Station.
- **Why this story:** the most-talked-about Calgary story of the past 48 hours (festival week, the game in two days), practical for everyone in the city (road changes, transit). Not in log.json. The other big stories (proposed budget, water restrictions) were either covered on Nov 9 and Nov 11 or are ongoing without a fresh turn today.
