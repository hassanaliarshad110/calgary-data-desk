## 2. What happened / why it matters / what happens next
- What happened: Bill 43 tabled Thursday. If passed: new bike lanes that remove or restrict road capacity for vehicles need the transportation minister's written approval; the minister can order existing lanes like that removed; municipalities can request exemptions, case by case.
- Why it matters: shifts decisions on some Calgary street space from City Hall to the province.
- What happens next: the bill still has to pass; details are to come in regulations. Calgary's mayor says he expects changes.
- Both sides, in their own terms: the minister (accountability, safety, congestion); Calgary's mayor (opposes provincial intervention, expects changes). No cost claims (sources conflict on who pays, so it is left out).
