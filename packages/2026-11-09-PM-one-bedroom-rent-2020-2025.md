## Methodology and limitations
- Boundary: Calgary CMA (census metropolitan area), not the City of Calgary alone. Narration says "the Calgary area"; on-screen says "Calgary CMA (metro area)".
- Series: CMHC Rental Market Survey, primary rental market, "Row / Apartment" view of Table 2.2.11, October of each year. Averages cover all surveyed units (not just new leases), so asking rents for vacant units can differ.
- Averages compare different samples of units each year; CMHC's same-sample percentage change for 2025 is suppressed ("**") on the Calgary dashboard, so the video does not claim rents fell in 2025, only that the average "levelled off".
- Nominal dollars, not adjusted for inflation.
- The October 2026 survey has not been published; October 2025 is the latest.
