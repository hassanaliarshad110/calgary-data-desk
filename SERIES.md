## Family names and Sakina's gag (Hassan, Nov 14, 2026)
Qasim and Sakina call their mother **Mama** (never "Ammi") and their father **Baba**. BABA is a new family member (see CHARACTER.md). Sakina is the **"crying girl"**: her fake-tears act makes the grown-ups give her what she wants — a running gag in most episodes, often used to help Qasim slip away. These apply to every future episode, script, caption, title and Short.
